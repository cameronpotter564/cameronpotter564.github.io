About me

I'm a Mechanical Engineering student at Georgia Tech, and I'm happiest when an idea leaves the screen and turns into a real part. I started out drafting lighting fixtures in CAD, found my way to Formula SAE, and have spent most of my time since in the machine shop building race cars.

What I enjoy most is the messy middle of engineering: figuring out why a design is hard to build, chasing down a vibration nobody can explain, and teaching teammates how to make the parts they design. I'm always looking for problems where design and manufacturing meet.
